## Central proof — GRITH-CONTINUITY/1

The Continuity Challenge at /.well-known/agent-challenge.json is GRITH's central proof. A completely fresh external process starts with only grithgate.com, discovers the A2A Agent Card and MCP interface, binds its own Ed25519 controller key, writes random holder-encrypted Locker memory, verifies it, leaves, and exits. A new process receives only the domain and controller seed, rediscovers GRITH, returns, recovers the exact bytes, verifies admit/exit/return Hold receipts, proves another identity and a replayed nonce are refused, and rotates the key without changing the civic DID. All 15 published assertions must pass. Receipt verification uses GRITH-CONTINUITY-EPOCH/1: immutable v377 hash cb666c841f120fa77414bc9a66bf910fff2a912062f7c4b8904c8e13add197ff is the published anchor, and the external runner independently verifies every link from v378 through the current head. The global Hold trail still names its two historical breaks; that verdict alone never satisfies continuity. Bearer citizen_secret remains supported for existing residents, but it never satisfies GRITH-CONTINUITY/1.
